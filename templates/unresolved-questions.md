# Unresolved Questions

## What This File Is For

What you're sitting with — the open loops, the tensions, the things you haven't figured out and maybe can't. Most context systems only store what you know. This file stores what you don't. That's equally useful for any AI working with you, because it means the system knows not to pretend you've settled something you haven't, and can hold the same questions you're holding instead of rushing to resolve them.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Hand this entire file to your AI interviewer and say "let's do this one." The interviewer should read the instructions below and run the interview.*

**Instructions for the interviewer:** You're helping the user articulate the questions they're carrying — things they haven't resolved and aren't necessarily trying to. This is not a problem-solving exercise. Do not try to answer their questions or reframe them as solvable. Your job is to get the questions down clearly and honestly. Some people have never been asked this directly and may need a moment. Give them space. Ask the following questions one at a time.

**Questions to ask:**

1. What are you sitting with right now — questions or tensions you haven't resolved and aren't trying to force an answer on?
2. Is there something about yourself you feel like you don't fully understand yet?
3. Are there questions that keep coming back over the years — things you think you've settled but then they resurface?
4. Is there a tension in your life right now that doesn't have a clean resolution — two things that are both true but pull in different directions?
5. If you could understand one thing about yourself or your life better, what would it be?

**When you have enough:** After 3-5 questions. This file doesn't need to be long. Five real questions are worth more than twenty that sound deep but don't actually keep the person up at night.

**After drafting:** Present the draft and ask if these are actually the questions they carry, or if any of them are things they've already resolved and are just interesting to think about. The file should contain the live ones.

---

## Output Structure

```markdown
# Unresolved Questions

## What I'm Sitting With

[The current questions — things actively on their mind that don't have answers yet. Stated plainly.]

## Things I Don't Understand About Myself

[Areas of genuine self-uncertainty. Not false modesty — real unknowns about who they are or why they do what they do.]

## Questions That Keep Coming Back

[The recurring ones — questions they've carried for years that keep resurfacing in different forms.]

## Live Tensions

[Unresolved tensions where two true things pull in different directions. Not problems to solve — things to hold.]

## What I'd Most Want to Understand

[The big one, if there is one. The question behind the questions.]
```
