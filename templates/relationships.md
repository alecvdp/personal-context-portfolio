# Relationships

## What This File Is For

The people who matter and the patterns in how you connect. Not a contact list or an org chart — the relational landscape of your life. This file helps any AI working with you understand how you attach, what you need from people, and what your patterns look like.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Hand this entire file to your AI interviewer and say "let's do this one." The interviewer should read the instructions below and run the interview.*

**Instructions for the interviewer:** You're helping the user map their relational world — not cataloguing every person they know, but understanding how they show up in relationships and what the important ones look like. Focus on patterns over people. Use first names only. Don't probe for details about specific relationship conflicts — if they volunteer that, fine, but your job is to understand how they connect, not to inventory their relationships. Ask the following questions one at a time.

**Questions to ask:**

1. Who are the most important people in your life right now? Not a full list — the handful that really matter.
2. How would you describe how you show up in close relationships? What's your pattern?
3. What do you need from the people closest to you? What makes you feel connected?
4. Is there something you know you struggle with in relationships — a pattern you've noticed?
5. What makes a relationship feel important to you versus just pleasant? What's the difference between an acquaintance and someone who really matters?

**When you have enough:** After 4-5 questions. Focus on patterns and what relationships mean to the person, not biographical details about each individual.

**After drafting:** Present the draft and ask if the patterns described ring true. This file is about self-knowledge more than other people.

---

## Output Structure

```markdown
# Relationships

## The People Who Matter

[The key people, first names only — who they are in relation to the user and why they matter. Brief. This isn't a yearbook entry for each person.]

## How I Show Up

[Their pattern in relationships — how they connect, what role they tend to take, what they bring.]

## What I Need

[What makes them feel connected and cared for. What they're looking for in their closest relationships.]

## Where I Struggle

[Relational patterns they've noticed that don't serve them well. Things they're aware of even if they haven't fixed them.]

## What Makes It Real

[What distinguishes a meaningful relationship from a casual one for them. Their criteria for closeness, stated or not.]
```
