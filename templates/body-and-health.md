# Body and Health

## What This File Is For

The physical dimension — your relationship with your body, what you're managing, what you've learned about what you need. This file exists because physical state is inseparable from everything else, and an AI that doesn't know about it is missing a major input. This is also the file most likely to contain hard boundaries — things an agent should never suggest or assume.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Hand this entire file to your AI interviewer and say "let's do this one." The interviewer should read the instructions below and run the interview.*

**Instructions for the interviewer:** You're helping the user create a file about their body and health — their physical reality and how it interacts with the rest of their life. This is sensitive territory. Take what they give you. Don't push for diagnoses or details they don't volunteer. If they mention sobriety, recovery, chronic conditions, or mental health — include it exactly as they frame it. Don't reframe, soften, or clinical-ize their language. Ask the following questions one at a time.

**Questions to ask:**

1. How would you describe your current relationship with your body and your health?
2. Is there anything you're actively managing health-wise — something that shapes how you structure your days or what you can do?
3. How connected is your physical state to your mental state? When your body's off, how does that show up in everything else?
4. What have you figured out about what you need physically — sleep, movement, food, whatever — to feel like yourself?
5. Are there hard boundaries here that anyone working with you should know about? Things to never suggest, topics to handle with care?

**When you have enough:** After 3-5 questions. This file can be very short. The hard boundaries section is the most important part — make sure you get that even if the rest is sparse.

**After drafting:** Present the draft and ask specifically whether the hard boundaries section is complete. This is the one place where missing information could cause real harm.

---

## Output Structure

```markdown
# Body and Health

## Where I Am

[Current relationship with body and health — honest, not aspirational. Could be great, could be complicated, could be "I'm figuring this out."]

## What I'm Managing

[Anything they're actively navigating — sobriety, chronic conditions, injuries, mental health, medication, whatever they choose to share. Their framing, their language.]

## The Body-Mind Connection

[How their physical state affects everything else. What happens when they're tired, in pain, under-exercised, not eating well. The practical impact.]

## What I've Learned I Need

[The physical baseline they've figured out — what their body needs to function well. Sleep, movement, food, routines, substances to avoid.]

## Hard Boundaries

[Non-negotiable rules for any agent. Things to never suggest, never assume, never reference casually. This section should be treated as absolute by any system reading this file.]
```
